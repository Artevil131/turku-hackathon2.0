# Context: If Insurance Could Do More

The second challenge we entered at JunctionXTurku 2026 (16–18 October),
by If Vahinkovakuutus Oyj. This file states the brief as given, what we
know about If, and what we still need to ask. Nothing technical is decided
here; choices go in DECISIONS.md.

## The brief, as given

> This is not simply a "build another insurance app" challenge. The
> difficult part is identifying a real human need where an insurance
> company can provide meaningful value without making insurance itself the
> centre of the experience. The best idea might prevent something from
> going wrong, make a life change easier, help someone make a better
> decision, connect useful services together, or create something
> completely different. AI, data and automation can absolutely be part of
> the solution, but technology is not the goal. The goal is something
> people would genuinely value, with a believable reason why it would also
> make sense for If as a business.

Resources: teams are encouraged to use public and open data: If's and
other insurers' published terms and conditions, weather and forecasting
data, public APIs, statistics and datasets. No customer or confidential
insurance data is given or needed.

| 25 % | (text duplicated in the brief; probably feasibility or implementation) | see question 1 below |

The brief as pasted repeats the business-value text under criteria 3 and
4. We read criterion 3 as feasibility or technical execution and 4 as

Two phrases carry weight: "connected to a meaningful life event" (criterion
1) and "without making insurance itself the centre" (the brief). The
concept in CONCEPT.md is built on both.

## What we know about If

- If P&C is the Nordic property and casualty insurer of Sampo Group,
  operating in Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and the Baltics. Sampo
  reports about 9 million customers group-wide and roughly 3.7 million
  private households in the four Nordic countries (2024 figure, Inderes);
  the Finnish share is not public in what we found (research/sources.md).
- In Finland If sells through its own channels and through Nordea's
  branches and app (partnership renewed 2020).
- P&C lines: home, car, travel, personal accident, pet, boat, business.
  Water leaks are the largest home-insurance cost in Finland: Finanssiala
  counted 34 800 leak damages and 171 M€ paid in 2021, more than fire
  (6 100 damages, 168 M€) and far more than burglary (21 400, 22 M€)
  (research/sources.md). Competitor LähiTapiola already gives
  policyholders leak sensors (with GROHE) to cut those claims, so
  "prevention as the product" is a line If's market already takes
  seriously.
- If publishes its terms and conditions and IPID sheets openly (if.fi), so
  "what does my policy actually cover at this event" can be answered from
  public documents.

## Questions for the judges and the If mentor

1. Criteria 3 and 4 are printed with the same text as criterion 2. What
   are they really?
2. Would If accept a product whose data never reaches If unless the
   customer chooses to share a confirmed life event? Is "we see nothing
   by default" a feature or a problem for them?
3. Is the target user an existing If customer, a prospect, or both? (Our
   answer: both; see CONCEPT.md.)
4. Which life events matter most to If commercially: moving, first car,
   first child, long travel, retirement, starting a business?
5. Does If have (or want) partner services around life events: movers,
   locksmiths, inspection stations, pet clinics? That changes the
   "connect useful services" part.
6. How far may we go into prevention advice that touches coverage
   ("your policy does not cover X at the new address")? Are the public
   T&Cs enough to say that reliably?
7. Deadline for the submission and the pitch format (minutes, demo live or
   video).
