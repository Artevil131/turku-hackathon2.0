## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone looked at the sky of your own data and found a few bright things. The app is the chart of that sky: evidence as stars, events as constellations, the next months as a plate you can read. It refuses the card stack of equal grey rectangles and the dashboard of tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Night-chart ground (deep navy, not black) with chalk-white ink, a cool slate for secondary ink, and one night-vision amber for the thing under the eye: selected state, the ringed object, the counter after a share. Warnings keep a distinct hot red-orange; teal is gone. Type: a light italic serif names events and sections (constellation names); a tabular-figure sans sets dates, counts and body. Material: hairline coordinate rules, dots sized on a fixed magnitude ramp (five sizes), faint figure lines; no fills, no shadows, no gradients. Surfaces are the chart itself; controls are standard web buttons and switches in chalk outline, the primary in amber fill. Light mode exists (a paper chart: cream ground, ink navy, amber unchanged) for daylight projectors; dark is the default scene.

STORY: Choose what the phone may read (standard switches). Press Check now: the sky fills with the window's signals as faint dots, and the ones that formed an event brighten and join into a constellation with a name and a date. Open an event: its constellation alone, the signals listed below as a timetable, the question and three answers above the evidence. Confirm: the journey, a plate of cards in the same ink. Share: the three fields leave the chart and the counter in the boundary strip turns amber. Novelty and the horizon are two more plates: what changed this month, and the next 90 days with the risks drawn on it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390×844. Top: a thin navy header with the wordmark in the serif and a Settings button. Then the sky plate, about 44% of the height: a canvas with faint coordinate hairlines, the window's snippets as magnitude-ramped dots placed by date (x) and source (y band), the fired events' signals brightened and joined by hairlines, each constellation labelled in italic serif with its date in tabular sans, the soonest event ringed in amber. Below: "Needs your answer" as the task heading in serif, the event rows as a timetable (date cell | title | arrow) in chalk on navy, then the full-width amber Check now. Bottom: the outlined boundary strip "Data leaving this phone 0 requests" and the three-tab navigation, unchanged in position. Signature interaction: Check now draws the dots first, then brightens and connects the constellations in reading order (600 ms, exponential ease-out); the share lifts the three rows out of the chart and the counter flips to 1 in amber. Reduced motion: dots and lines appear in place.
