# What if…: speaker notes

For the deck (`pitch/what-if-deck.pdf`), about 3 minutes. Each slide has one point, in bold: say that in
your own words, and use the lines below as a guide, not a script. The numbers you need are here, so the
slides can stay short. Longer answers to judges' questions are in `pitch/QA.md`.

Before you start: open https://what-if.what-if.workers.dev in a browser tab, next to the slides.

## 1. What if… (10 s)

**Who we are, and the idea in one line.**

"Hi, we're Artem, Aapo and Vaishnav, and this is What if…. Insurers find out how it ends when the claim
comes in. What if… tells you before you sign."

## 2. Imagine (25 s)

**A real person at a big decision, and nobody tells them who pays.**

"Imagine you're 18 and buying your first car. You've found a 2009 BMW for €6,900, and it looks great. But
what should you ask the seller? What will it really cost? And if something goes wrong, who pays?

Almost half of these BMWs failed their last inspection. And if the gearbox goes, insurance doesn't pay. You
do.

Or you're buying your first flat, in a block from 1974. If the pipe renovation starts next year, your share
is about €51,600, and that isn't insurance either.

Today, you find out after you've signed."

## 3. Live demo (60 s)

**Paste an ad, and in seconds you know what to ask, what it costs and who pays.**

Switch to the browser tab. Under "Or try an example", tap **Used BMW 320d**, then **Check this ad**.

  Traficom's inspection results for this exact model and year."

## 4. Business model (40 s)

**The free check brings people in, what they get with If keeps them, and If earns from better
customers.**

"So how does If make money? Not from fees. From better customers.

The check is free for everyone, because it reaches people at the exact moment they need insurance: you
can't drive that BMW home without traffic insurance. One tap, and the If quote is already filled in, so
winning that customer costs almost nothing.

After signing, If customers get the reasons to stay. A reminder to check the winter tyres' tread. A
reminder to turn the water off before a week away; leaks are the biggest home insurance cost in Finland,
€171 million in 2021. A logbook, huoltokirja, for the car or the home. And a guarantee behind every
who-pays label.

At the next life event, a home or a family, they check again and add a policy. A customer is worth the
years they stay, times premiums minus claims minus costs. What if… moves all four, and it brings the
customers who bring If the most money: flat buyers first."

## 5. Why it works (25 s)

**It's real, and people can trust it.**

"It's live today, on any phone. Every number has a source: Traficom, Isännöintiliitto, Finanssiala,
Finnish law and If's own terms.

When our rules can't read an ad, our own AI model reads it, and every fact has to quote the ad. In Aapo's
test, 161 of 162 fields came out right, and none were invented.

It's private: only the ad is sent, screenshots only after a check, and nothing is stored. The details go
to If when the person asks for a quote.

And it's honest about who pays, even when the answer isn't insurance. That's why people will trust it."

## 6. How people find it (20 s)

**They find it where they already are, and every path ends at an If quote.**

"People find it where they already are. If's own customers, in the If app, at their next car or home.
People searching 'is a 2009 BMW reliable?': pages built from the same data lead straight into a check.
Where they sign: landlords, housing managers, car dealers and estate agents. And marketplaces last, paid
per policy.

Every path ends in the same place: an If quote, already filled in."

## 7. What If needs to launch it (15 s)

**Four concrete steps, then the closing line.**

"To launch it, If needs four things: connect the hand-over to its quote, add its own claims data so the
risks are exact, put the reminders and the logbook in the If app, and a legal review.

What if… See the what-ifs before you sign. Thank you."

## Short on time

About 3 minutes 15 seconds in all. For 2 minutes: in the demo, show only the clutch, the €12,000 and who

## If a judge asks

- **What happens to the data?** "Only the ad's text is sent, without phone numbers or email addresses, and
  screenshots only after a check. There's no account and no tracking, nothing is stored, and the server
  logs only errors. The details go to If when the person asks for a quote. A legal review is on the
  launch list."
- **Won't people check for free and buy cheaper insurance elsewhere?** "Some will, and a check costs
  almost nothing. If wins the moment: first in line, already filled in, honest, and with a guarantee that
  only comes with an If policy."
- **Won't it attract only the risky buyers?** "The price follows the same facts the buyer confirms:
  building year, pipe renovation, car model. The tool also steers people to the safer choice. Using the
  tool never changes a price."
- **Can the AI make things up?** "Every field it returns must quote the ad, or it's dropped: 161 of 162
  right, none invented. Costs and who pays never come from the AI."
- **Do the reminders really cut claims?** "They go after the biggest claim types: leaks and winter
  driving. How much they cut is something If can measure with its own claims data."
