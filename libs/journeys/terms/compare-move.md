# Home insurance during a move: three providers side by side

From each provider's own public page, read 2026-10-17. If is one of the three and is labelled as the provider of this challenge in the app.

| Provider | Cover in old and new home during a move | Must the customer move the policy? | Source |
|----------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|--------|
| If | Three months, when the contents are insured at If; includes the surfaces and fixtures of both flats | Yes, in Omat sivut or by phone; it does not move by itself | https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/kotivakuutus/muuttaminen |
| LähiTapiola | Two months, when moving within Finland and both homes are in your possession | Yes; remember to move it and give the new address | https://www.lahitapiola.fi/henkilo/vakuutukset/kotivakuutus/kotivakuutus-ja-muutto/ |
| OP (Pohjola) | Up to two months for the items and parts of both flats | Yes, in op.fi; the new policy must be active on the day of the move | https://www.op.fi/en/private-customers/insurance/home-insurance/moving-home-and-home-insurance |

All three also say: moving between a flat and a detached house, or owning two homes for a while, needs insurance on both properties; a flat policy never pays for a detached house or the other way round (LähiTapiola, OP).

This table compares what the pages say about moving only. It is not a price or full-cover comparison, and the app must not present it as one.
