# If, travel insurance: what the long-trip journey relies on

Paraphrased from If's public documents, read 2026-10-17.

## Matkavakuutusehdot, Ehto MAT 120.8, in force from 1.1.2026

Source: https://www.if.fi/globalassets/fi/pdf/ehdot/matkavakuutusehdot.pdf

| Clause | What it says (paraphrase) | Used by journey item |
|--------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| 1.2 Voimassaoloaika | Continuous travel cover applies to trips of the length stated in the policy; a fixed-term policy applies for the period stated in the policy. | `long-trip-cover` |
| 2.1 Vakuutetut | Insured are those named in the policy who are covered by the Finnish health insurance act and live permanently in Finland. Someone who stays abroad for more than six months of a calendar year is not counted as living permanently in Finland. | `long-trip-cover` |
| 3.1.1 Korvaus hoitokuluista | An illness that existed before the trip and suddenly worsens is paid only as acute first-aid-type treatment given within one week at the destination. | `long-trip-health` |
| 3.1.2 Korvaus matkan peruuntuessa | Cancellation is paid only if the travel insurance was taken at the latest three days before the trip starts, and the reason did not appear before the cover was taken or the trip booked. | `long-trip-cover` |

Used by: `long-trip-cover`, `long-trip-health`.
Home while away: see `if-home.md`, clause 5.5 (3).
