| D-017 | 2026-10-17 | Listing analyst (`@ahead/listing`): an ad the user hands to the app (car or home) is read by Qwen3.8-27B (NVFP4, NInfer) on chili's RTX 5090 through its OpenAI-compatible endpoint, thinking off, temperature 0. Every field must quote the ad and the value must be written in its quote, or it is dropped. Code, not the model, adds the risk map and costs (`risks.ts`, named sources). This is an exception to D-002 for one thing only: the ad's text leaves the phone, and only when the user taps to analyse it; no identity, snippet or vector goes with it, and NInfer's request log keeps timings, not text. The PWA wiring is recorded in D-018 | A 0.4–1 GB model on the phone was the alternative; the 27B already runs on chili, needs no download, reads Finnish and images, and the benchmark (benchmarks/listing) gave 118/120 fields right, none wrong, none invented, 1.6 s an ad | Aapo |
